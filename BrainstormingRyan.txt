# BRAINSTORMING

Ryan's Exercise Book Rewrite:

Engineer Part of the game:
Involves one of the players being "in" a submarine controlling and repairing it to keep the submarine in working order. 
They would be using an interactive board with missions on it. But before doing the task they must navigate to the room that has the problem.

Engineer Missions include:
Realignment of the ship: Press between two buttons to either change its direction up or down. 
Turn the valve: Turn a valve to stop gas from leaking.
Weld the hull: Move a "welding torch" across the screen to follow a line to weld it shut.
Pump The gas: use the pump to refuel the engine.
Restart the engine: input a sequence of buttons to restart the engine.
Pump The water: Pump the water out of the ship.
Fix the bolts: little bolts appear around the screen that you have to hammer in to make sure they don't burst out. 

Fisher Part of the game: 
They will be fishing for rare fish in the deep ocean, using their harpoon gun they have to real in fish by completing mini games.
They will sell these fish to buy upgrades for both the engineer and the Fisher at checkpoints. 

Fisher Mission include (reeling the fish in):
Rhythm game.
Keeping your cursor inside of a area for a certain amount of time.
